    How we work

    Built for the chemist's day

    • 01

      Order by WhatsApp

      Pick items from the rate list and send the whole order to us in one message. No app, no login, no typing the list twice.

    • 02

      Same-day city delivery

      Orders confirmed before 4 PM reach shops inside Kanpur the same evening. Outstation parcels go by the next morning's transport.

    • 03

      Cold-chain handling

      Insulins, vaccines and other 2–8°C items are stored in monitored refrigeration and moved in insulated boxes with ice packs.

    • 04

      Genuine stock, full papers

      Every consignment comes from the company depot or authorised C&F, with batch number, expiry and a GST invoice on each bill.

    • 05

      Expiry & breakage support

      Near-expiry stock is taken back on the company's standard terms and credited against your next purchase.

    • 06

      Institutional supply

      Rate contracts and scheduled monthly supply for nursing homes, hospitals and clinics, with consolidated monthly billing.
