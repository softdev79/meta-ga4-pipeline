About the Advocate

Nearly five decades at the bar, one standard of counsel.

Dr. Om Prakash Gupta is a Senior Advocate with more than forty-seven years of continuous practice. His chamber, Genius Senior Advocate & Associates, advises individuals, families, landholders, employees, traders and small businesses — and appears for them at every stage, from the first notice through trial, appeal and revision.

The chamber’s approach is deliberately plain: an honest reading of the case at the first meeting, a realistic view of time and cost, and documentation prepared carefully enough that it holds up years later. Clients are told what the law permits, not what they would prefer to hear.

  • Practice before the Hon’ble High Court of Judicature at Allahabad
  • Appearances before District & Sessions Courts, tribunals and revenue authorities
  • Drafting, vetting and registration of deeds, agreements and settlements
  • Consultation in Hindi and English

Practice Areas

Matters the chamber handles

If your matter is not listed, please still ask — the chamber will tell you frankly whether it is the right place for it.

Civil Litigation

Suits for declaration, possession, injunction, specific performance, partition, recovery and damages; appeals and revisions.

Criminal Law

Bail and anticipatory bail, quashing petitions, trial defence, complaints, appeals and revisions in criminal matters.

Property & Land

Title examination, sale deeds, gift and partition deeds, mutation, boundary and possession disputes, builder disputes.

Revenue & Tenancy

Proceedings before Tehsildar, SDM and Commissioner; land records, varasat, chakbandi and revenue appeals.

Family & Matrimonial

Divorce, restitution, maintenance, custody and guardianship, domestic violence proceedings, mutual settlements.

Service & Labour

Departmental enquiries, termination and suspension, promotion and seniority, pension and retiral dues, labour disputes.

Writs & Constitutional

Writ petitions under Article 226 against State action, public authorities and statutory bodies; contempt proceedings.

Cheque Bounce & Recovery

Complaints under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, legal notices, money suits and execution.

Motor Accident Claims

Compensation claims before the MACT, insurance disputes and appeals against inadequate awards.

Consumer Protection

Complaints before District, State and National Commissions against deficiency in service and unfair trade practice.

Succession & Wills

Wills, succession certificates, probate and letters of administration, family settlements and inheritance disputes.

Agreements & Documentation

Drafting and vetting of agreements, power of attorney, lease and rent deeds, notices, affidavits and undertakings.

How It Works

From your first call to your first hearing

  1. 1

    Request an appointment

    Fill the form below or call the chamber. Mention your city and the nature of the dispute in one or two lines.

  2. 2

    Confirmation

    The chamber confirms your slot on WhatsApp or by phone, and tells you which papers to bring.

  3. 3

    Consultation

    Your papers are read, the position in law is explained, and the realistic options, timeline and cost are set out.

  4. 4

    Representation

    If you decide to proceed, drafting begins, the matter is filed, and you are kept informed at every date.
